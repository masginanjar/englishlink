The Garuda Scholarship (Beasiswa Garuda) funds Indonesian school leavers to take a bachelor's degree abroad. It is run by the Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Technology and financed by LPDP, the state education endowment. If you teach, counsel or parent a grade 12 student in Indonesia, this is what the official 2027 guidebook says as of 23 November 2026.

## What are the key dates?

| Stage | Date in the official guidebook |
|---|---|
| Online application | 12 November 2026 to 4 January 2027 |
| Scholastic Aptitude Test (TBS), online | 18 to 22 January 2027 |
| Administrative selection results | 5 February 2027 |
| Placement interview | To be announced |

Dates can change, so check the official site before submitting.

## Who can apply?

The basic rules for the overseas track:

1. Indonesian citizen, shown by an ID card, family card, child identity card or passport.
2. Currently in grade 12 at an SMA, SMK or MA in the 2026/2027 school year.
3. An average of at least 85 across school reports for semesters 1 to 4 on the national curriculum. Alternatives: IB predicted score of 36, A-Level predicted AAA, or ACT 28.
4. An IQ score of at least 120 from an applied psychology unit at a university under the ministry with accreditation of at least "Baik" (Good). The result must be no more than 2 years old on the closing date.
5. English, one of: IELTS 6.5 with no band below 6.0, TOEFL iBT 86, or TOEFL ITP 550.
6. SAT of at least 1,200. Applicants without it, or below it, must sit the TBS and score at least 1,000.

Applicants also choose 2 of 10 priority themes, such as health, energy, digitalisation or food security.

## Document checklist

Summarised from the guidebook's upload table, grouped by who issues each item.

**From home**
- [ ] ID card, child identity card or passport
- [ ] Family card (Kartu Keluarga)

**From the school**
- [ ] Scanned reports for semesters 1 to 4, merged into one PDF
- [ ] Recommendation letter from the principal
- [ ] Principal's decree for student council experience, if any

**From test providers**
- [ ] Valid English test certificate
- [ ] IQ test certificate
- [ ] SAT certificate, if available

**From a government health facility**
- [ ] Physical and mental health certificate, including a colour vision check
- [ ] Drug-free certificate

**Completed inside the application**
- [ ] Personal data and profile
- [ ] Personal statement
- [ ] Agreement to the applicant's declaration on submission

**Supporting, not mandatory**
- [ ] Achievement certificates from the last 3 years, merged into one PDF
- [ ] Valid social assistance card such as PIP or KJP
- [ ] Parents' proof of income, 3 months of bank statements and 3 months of electricity bills

Applicants with disabilities add a disability certificate and a signed parental consent letter. Templates are in the guidebook.

## Which items take longest?

Start with the items someone else has to issue:

1. Book the IQ test and the English test, because test dates and certificate release are outside the family's control.
2. Ask the school for the recommendation and report scans before the year-end break. The 4 January deadline lands right after the holidays.
3. Get the health and drug-free certificates from a government facility.
4. Draft the personal statement while waiting.

## Do the documents need to be in English?

The application guidebook does not mention any translation requirement for school reports, the family card or other documents. For the application stage, upload what is asked for.

Things usually change after selection, when the student deals with the destination university and a visa. Indonesian school reports, diplomas and birth certificates are issued in Indonesian, and admissions or visa offices often ask for English versions. The required language and form of certification are set by that university and embassy, so follow their official instructions. If English versions are requested, see our service for [academic documents](/en/services/academic-documents/) and our overview of [documents for studying abroad](/en/articles/documents-for-studying-abroad/).

## What does the scholarship cover?

The guidebook lists two groups of funding, without amounts:

- Education funds: tuition, application fees and a book allowance.
- Support funds: travel, visa application, health insurance, arrival costs, monthly living costs and emergency costs.

## FAQ

### When does the Garuda Scholarship 2027 application close?
The official guidebook gives 12 November 2026 to 4 January 2027 for the overseas track. Check the official site, as dates can change.

### Can a student apply without an SAT score?
Yes. Applicants with no SAT, or a score below 1,200, must take the scholarship's Scholastic Aptitude Test and score at least 1,000.

### Do school reports need to be translated to apply?
The 2027 application guidebook does not mention translation. It asks for scanned reports for semesters 1 to 4 in a single PDF.
