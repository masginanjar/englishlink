If you are applying for a visa, enrolling at a university or marrying across borders, the phrase *sworn translation* will appear sooner or later. It sounds heavy, but the idea is simple.

## So what does "sworn" mean?

A sworn translator (*penerjemah tersumpah*) has taken an oath before a state official. Because of that oath, the translator takes legal responsibility for stating that the translation matches the source document.

A sworn translation usually carries three things:

- **A statement** that the translation is faithful to the original.
- **The translator's signature.**
- **The translator's stamp.**

## When is it required?

One rule decides everything: **what the receiving party asks for.**

| Purpose | Documents often requested |
|---|---|
| Study or scholarships abroad | Diploma, transcript, birth certificate |
| Visas and residence permits | Birth certificate, family card, marriage certificate, bank statement |
| Marriage with a foreign national | Birth certificate, single-status letter |
| Business | Company deeds, contracts, powers of attorney |
| Foreign documents used in Indonesia | Overseas diplomas and certificates |

## When is a regular translation enough?

For a CV, an email, a presentation or anything read only internally, a regular translation is usually fine. If you are unsure, ask the recipient directly: "Does the translation need to be done by a sworn translator?"

## An everyday comparison

Imagine a friend who speaks excellent English translates your birth certificate. It may be perfectly accurate. The problem is that the embassy officer does not know your friend and has no way to rely on the result.

A sworn translation works like a certified true copy: the content is the same, but someone with recognised standing has signed and taken responsibility for it. The officer only needs to look at the statement, signature and stamp.

## FAQ

### Is a sworn translator the same as a notary?
No. A sworn translator translates documents. A notary draws up deeds and certifies signatures. Some purposes need both.

### Is an Indonesian sworn translation accepted everywhere?
Not automatically. Each country and institution sets its own rules, and some ask for an apostille or legalization on top. Follow the official requirements of the recipient.
