A diploma and transcript are almost always the first documents a university or scholarship provider asks for. The process is not complicated once you know the order.

## Step by step

1. **Read the recipient's requirements.** Do they ask for a sworn translation? Legalization or an apostille?
2. **Gather the documents.** Diploma, transcript and, if you have one, a graduation letter.
3. **Photograph or scan clearly.** Front and back, all four corners visible.
4. **Send them for review.** You will receive a cost and time estimate.
5. **Check the result.** Compare name, degree and grades with the originals.

## What you must check

- **Name spelling.** It should match your passport exactly. One letter can cause trouble.
- **Programme and degree names.** Tell the translator if your university has an official English name for them.
- **The back page.** Legalization stamps and notes on the reverse are translated too.
- **The grading scale.** Do not let the GPA scale note get cropped.

## Mistakes that get files sent back

1. **Translating the diploma but forgetting the transcript.** Many universities want both as a set.
2. **A name that differs from the passport.** The translation follows the diploma, so prepare supporting documents if the university asks.
3. **Leaving out the legalized page.** Stamps and signatures on the back are part of the document.
4. **Starting the translation after the application portal opens.** Uploading documents is often the first step.
5. **Assuming one translation fits every purpose.** Some recipients have their own format or extra requirements.

## A safe order for scholarship hunters

- **Well ahead:** collect the diploma, transcript and letters. Check name spelling across all of them.
- **When the checklist is published:** mark which documents need translating and which need legalization or an apostille.
- **Right after:** send the documents for review and a quote, stating the closing date.
- **After receiving the result:** verify name, degree and grades, then upload.

With this order you still have room if something needs fixing.

## If your diploma is from overseas

The reverse direction works too. An English-language diploma to be used in Indonesia, for example for degree recognition or a job application, can be translated into Indonesian.

## FAQ

### My diploma is already bilingual. Do I still need a translation?
It depends on the recipient. Some accept the university's bilingual diploma, others still ask for a sworn translation. Ask them first.

### Are the diploma and transcript quoted separately?
They are separate documents with different page counts. Send both together so the quote covers everything.

### Can I send a phone photo?
Yes, as long as it is bright, sharp and uncropped.
