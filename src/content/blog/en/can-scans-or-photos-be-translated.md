The short answer is **yes**. You do not have to carry a folder of papers to an office to get started.

## What makes a good photo

- **Bright and even.** Shoot near a window and keep your hand's shadow out.
- **Straight from above.** A tilted shot bends the text.
- **All corners visible.** Nothing cropped.
- **No glare.** Laminated documents reflect lamps, so shift the angle slightly.
- **One page per photo.** Easier to read than a collage.

A scanner app on your phone usually gives a cleaner result than the plain camera.

## Before and after

| The photo that causes delays | The photo that works right away |
|---|---|
| Taken at an angle while standing | Taken straight down with the paper on a table |
| Phone shadow across the page | Light from the side, no shadow |
| Top corner cropped, document number missing | All four corners in frame |
| Sent through an app that shrinks images | Sent as a "document" or original file |

A small tip people miss: on WhatsApp, send the file as a **Document** rather than a regular photo. That way it is not compressed and small print stays readable.

## Accepted file types

PDF, JPG and PNG all work. For official digital documents such as an e-diploma, send the original file instead of a screenshot, because the quality is better.

## What if part of it is blurry?

A translator must not guess. Names, dates and numbers that cannot be read will be queried or a new photo requested. That is not being difficult. It protects you from a typo in an important document.

## One-minute checklist before sending

- [ ] Every page is photographed, including backs with stamps or notes.
- [ ] Names, dates and numbers are readable without heavy zooming.
- [ ] Pages are in the right order.
- [ ] File names are clear, for example `diploma-front` and `diploma-back`.
- [ ] You have said what the document is for.

## What about originals?

For review and a quote, a photo or scan is enough. Some institutions have their own rules, so ask them two things:

1. Must the translation be attached to a copy of the source document?
2. Is a digital result accepted, or must it be printed with a wet stamp?

## FAQ

### Can a screenshot be translated?
Yes if it is readable, though the original file or a direct photo is better.

### My document is worn or torn. What happens?
It can still be reviewed. Missing or unreadable parts are noted in the translation, never filled in by guesswork.

### Are my files safe?
Documents are used only for the translation work and are not shared with anyone outside that purpose.
