A good translation still deserves one more read from you. Not out of distrust, but because **nobody knows your personal details better than you do**. Five minutes of checking can save weeks of delay.

## 1. Names

- Match the spelling to your **passport**, letter by letter.
- Watch names with many variants, such as Muhammad, Mohammad and Muhamad.
- Check the names of parents and spouse as well.
- Names are not translated. "Dewi Lestari" stays "Dewi Lestari".

## 2. Dates

Date formats differ between countries. `03/04/2026` may mean 3 April or 4 March. That is why a careful translation writes the month in letters, for example *3 April 2026*.

Check the date of birth, the issue date and the graduation or marriage date.

## 3. Numbers

- ID number, certificate number, diploma number, passport number.
- Figures in transcripts and bank statements.
- Look-alike digits: 1 and 7, 0 and 8, 5 and 6.

## Mini quiz: can you spot the difference?

| In the original | In another document |
|---|---|
| Muhamad Rizky Pratama | Muhammad Rizky Pratama |
| 12/03/1999 | 03/12/1999 |
| 3275014507990006 | 3275014507990008 |

**Answers:** row one has a different number of the letter "m". Row two can be read as 12 March or 3 December depending on the country's format. Row three differs in the last digit.

If that took more than a few seconds, that is normal. The eye tends to read what it expects, not what is written. Checking works best slowly, one line at a time.

## The five-minute checklist

1. Put the original and the translation side by side.
2. Read every name quietly aloud, letter by letter.
3. Point at each date and match them one by one.
4. Read each number from back to front. It forces the eye to slow down.
5. If anything differs, photograph that part and send it to the translator.

## Why spelling matters so much

Institutions abroad compare your documents with each other. If the name on a translated certificate differs by one letter from the passport, they may treat it as two different people and ask for more paperwork.

## When is the best time to check?

Not the moment the result arrives, while you are rushing. Set aside five calm minutes, ideally on a screen larger than a phone or on paper. If you can, have someone read the original aloud while you follow the translation.

## FAQ

### My birth certificate and passport really do differ. What now?
The translation must follow the source, so the difference will remain. Tell the translator early and ask the receiving institution what supporting documents they want.

### I found a typo. What should I do?
Contact the translator, point out the part and attach a photo so it can be checked against the source.

### Are degree titles translated?
Academic titles are usually written as in the source, sometimes with an equivalent noted. Tell the translator if your university has an official one.
