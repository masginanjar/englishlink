People used to carry a folder to a translation office. Today the whole thing can be done from your sofa. Here is the order, with the small details that keep it painless.

## Step 1: prepare the files

Photograph or scan every page, including backs that carry stamps. Give files clear names. If you have many documents, group them by type so nothing gets mixed up.

## Step 2: give the context

Three pieces of information save a lot of back-and-forth:

1. What the document is for and who will receive it.
2. The direction, Indonesian to English or the reverse.
3. When you need it in hand.

## Step 3: read the quote carefully

A good quote states the total cost, the expected time and the form of the result. If something is unclear, ask now and not after the work is done.

## Step 4: agree on the format

| Format | Best for |
|---|---|
| Digital (PDF) | Uploading to a university portal, sending by email |
| Printed | Handing over in person at an embassy or office |
| Both | When you are not sure what the recipient wants |

## Step 5: check before you use it

Compare names, dates and numbers with the original. Five minutes here can spare you weeks.

## Think of it like a food delivery order

Write "chicken, spice level 2, no garlic sauce" and you get what you wanted. Write only "chicken" and the kitchen decides. A translation order works the same way: the clearer your notes on purpose and format, the less likely you are to go back and forth.

## The small mistake that causes delays

Most delays happen before the translation starts: files arrive in pieces, the back page follows a day later, or the purpose is mentioned only after the result is finished. Send everything in one go with a short note. One tidy message is processed faster than ten scattered ones.

## Signs of a provider you can rely on

- They review the document before naming a figure.
- They ask when something is unreadable.
- They explain what you will receive, not just the price.
- They show a business identity and an address.

## FAQ

### Is it safe to send personal documents by chat?
Send them only to the official number listed on the provider's website, and make sure they explain how your documents are handled.

### What if I live in another city or abroad?
That is fine. Digital results can be sent anywhere, and printed results can be delivered as agreed.

### Do I pay before the document is reviewed?
Review and the quote come first. Payment is discussed only after you accept the offer.
