A translation provider is often chosen in a hurry because a deadline is close. Nine short questions can filter out most problems. Keep this list and use it in your first chat.

## About cost and time

1. **"What exactly does this price include?"** So nothing extra appears later.
2. **"When can I expect the result?"** Ask for a clear estimate and compare it with your deadline.
3. **"If I need it sooner, is that possible?"** Better to know now than to hope.

## About the result

4. **"What form does the result come in?"** Digital, printed or both.
5. **"Is this translation suitable for the institution I am submitting to?"** Name the institution.
6. **"What if a name or number is written incorrectly?"** Ask how to report it and what happens next.

## About your documents

7. **"Who sees my documents?"** A fair question, especially for financial and legal papers.
8. **"Do I need to hand over the original?"** For review, a photo or scan is usually enough.
9. **"What should I prepare to keep things smooth?"** The answer shows how well they understand the job.

## Reading the answers

| Reassuring | Worth a second thought |
|---|---|
| "Send the document first, we will review it and come back with a figure" | A price given without seeing the document |
| "This part is hard to read, could you retake the photo?" | Never asking anything |
| "You will receive a PDF and a print, as the institution requires" | "It will be fine" with no explanation |
| A stated business identity and address | Only a mobile number with no business name |

## Why asking is not a nuisance

A good provider welcomes questions, because they help them understand what you need. If someone seems irritated by a simple question, treat that as useful information before you spend money.

## Keep a record

Save a screenshot or copy the key answers into your notes, especially on cost, time and format. Not out of suspicion, but so both sides hold the same reference. If something later differs from what was agreed, you can simply point to it.

## The quick version

If you only have time for three, choose these: what is included, when it will be done, and what happens if something is wrong.

## FAQ

### Is it reasonable to ask several providers for a quote?
Yes. Compare not only the figure but also how clearly they answer.

### Should I give my real deadline?
Yes. An honest deadline helps the provider plan realistically.

### What if I do not know which type of translation I need?
Send the checklist from the receiving institution. It usually makes the answer obvious.
